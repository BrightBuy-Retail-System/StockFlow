Project 2 - Retail Inventory and Online Order Management System 
BrightBuy is a medium-scale consumer electronics retail chain based in Texas. To expand 
its presence and respond to market competition from online giants, BrightBuy has 
decided to launch a digital commerce platform for online product browsing, ordering, 
and delivery within Texas. 
Your team has been hired to design the backend database system for the first phase of 
this platform. In this phase, the platform will only support a limited catalogue (e.g., 
electronic gadgets and toys) with a focus on proper stock keeping, order processing, and 
basic delivery integration. 
Since database reliability is crucial for product availability, billing, and customer 
satisfaction, BrightBuy management has prioritised database design. Your system must 
include an UI interface. 
The business and system requirements are as follows: 
• BrightBuy operates a central warehouse  where all stock is maintained. Each 
product belongs to one or more categories (e.g., Mobiles, Audio Devices, Toys), 
and may have one or more variants (e.g., colour, memory size). The variant 
determines the final price and stock availability. 
• Some products have only one default variant. Products are assigned a unique SKU 
at the warehouse level. 
• Each variant has a stock quantity that is decremented upon order confirmation. 
• Users can browse as guests  or register as customers . Only registered 
customers can check out their cart and place an order. 
• Each order contains one or more items, each mapped to a specific product 
variant and quantity. Orders must contain delivery and payment details. 
• On checkout, the system should validate available stock and update inventory 
accordingly. Inventory updates must be atomic with order placement to avoid 
stock mismatches. 
• BrightBuy offers two delivery modes: 
o Store Pickup 
o Standard Delivery 
• It supports two payment methods: 
o Cash on Delivery 
o Card Payment

• Estimated delivery time is calculated based on stock availability and destination 
city. 
o Main cities: 5 days if in stock 
o Other cities: 7 days if in stock 
o Add 3 days to both if item is out of stock at the time of order 
The management requires the following reports from the system: 
1. Quarterly sales report for a given year 
2. Top-selling products in a given period 
3. Category-wise total number of orders 
4. Delivery time estimates for upcoming orders 
5. Customer-wise order summary and payment status 
Task 
Your task is to model the database design to encapsulate these requirements. It should 
consider all entities and relationships given in the description. Moreover, you need to 
identify the places where procedures, functions, and triggers  can be employed to 
guarantee ACID properties. Foreign keys and primary keys must be set to maintain 
consistency. Indexing should be done when necessary. 
Additionally, you must understand the domain by reading related material and take 
assumptions when not explicitly provided (e.g., category hierarchy, variant rules, delivery 
cost structure). The database must be populated with at least 40 products, with variants 
and at least 10 different categories.
